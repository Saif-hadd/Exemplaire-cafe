# Accounting and financial integrity

- Money is stored as integer MAD minor units in all new operational tables (100 = 1 MAD). Existing menu prices remain `numeric(10,2)` for compatibility. Postgres recalculates prices, verifies availability and modifiers, and snapshots names, categories, notes, modifiers and unit prices into order items. The guest's displayed expected total must match the database total before a new order is accepted.
- Tax is an **additional** configured rate in basis points, rounded once per order using numeric arithmetic. Default tax is zero. Have the restaurant's accountant confirm the correct configuration before activation. Historical order tax is immutable after creation.
- Gross sales are served order subtotals before discounts, excluding tax. No discount system is configured, so net sales equal gross sales. A sale is recognized at the first `served` transition; completing it on a different day does not move recognition. Submitted, accepted, preparing, and ready orders are not recognized sales.
- Outstanding balance is the total of all noncancelled/nonrejected orders minus recorded payments plus refunds. This includes unserved orders and all visits, independently of report date filters. It is a current operational balance, not an historical as-of receivables report.
- Collections are receipts confirmed during the selected business dates, less refunds recorded during those dates. Cash, physical terminal, and other methods are separate. An unpaid order contributes zero collected money.
- Payment confirmation uses a transaction and a visit row lock. The amount must be a positive integer within the remaining balance. Unique idempotency keys prevent retry duplication. Allocations are written oldest-order-first in that transaction. Closing, status transitions, and payments acquire visit locks consistently.
- Receipt records, allocations, status history, and adjustments cannot be directly changed or deleted by staff API callers. Staff identity comes from `auth.uid()`. Sensitive operations and menu/settings changes have audit records.
- Cancellation or rejection requires a reason and is allowed only before service. Allocated payments must be refunded before cancellation. Cancelled/rejected orders are excluded from sales and balances. Existing item snapshots remain intact when products are edited, archived, or deleted by a database administrator.
- Refunds are owner-only, full-receipt, physically returned amounts, with a reason and an immutable adjustment. A refund does not imply that the underlying sale was cancelled. It reopens the allocated balance. A historical refunded visit can receive a corrective payment without enabling guest access or mixing the current table visit.
- Reports use the configured IANA timezone, initially `Africa/Casablanca`. A business day runs from local midnight to the following local midnight, including timezone offset changes. Order count refers to orders created during the period; completed count refers to those orders currently completed. Preparation/service averages use status-history timestamps.
- A daily close records expected net cash, physically counted cash (excluding float and unrelated movements), variance, a financial snapshot, staff identity, notes and timestamp. It takes database share locks while capturing its snapshot. A date can be closed once. Later receipts/refunds affect live reports but do not overwrite the closing. A closing does not settle outstanding table visits.

This is an operational restaurant ledger, not a general ledger or tax-filing package. Partial refunds, arbitrary discounts, split tender attribution per diner, opening-float movements and jurisdiction-specific fiscal receipts are not implemented.
